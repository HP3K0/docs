### Properties
<ResponseField name="_id" type="string">Unique identifier for the collection transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="channel" type="string" default="API">
Channel used to initiate the collection transaction.

Available values: `API`, `WEB`, `USSD`
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="customerId" type="string">Identifier of the customer/merchant associated with the transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="customerType" type="string">
Type of customer associated with the transaction.

Available values: `merchants`, `customers`, `donations`
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="amount" type="number">Amount requested for collection.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="actualAmount" type="number">Actual amount credited to merchant account</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="charges" type="number">Charges applied to the transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="transaction_type" type="string" default="RM"><mark>Type</mark> of transaction. For collection, this value is "RM". [See transaction type reference.](/concepts/transactions)</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string"><Tooltip headline="Transaction status" tip="The authoritative field of a transaction's status. Account credit actions are taken based on this value." cta="Read full reference" href="/concepts/transactions">Status</Tooltip> of transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="description" type="string">Description of the collection transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="charge_type" type="string">
<mark>Party charged</mark> for the transaction. [See charge type reference](/concepts/transactions)

Available values: `merchant`, `customer`, `split`
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">Date the collection transaction was created, in ISO timestamp format.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string">Date the collection transaction was last updated, in ISO timestamp format.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment_account_name" type="string">Name on the payment account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment_account_number" type="string">Number of the payment account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="currency" type="string" default="GHS">Currency of the transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment_account_issuer" type="string"><Tooltip headline="Issuers on PeoplesPay" tip="Issuers are the business entities that own payment or recipient accounts (mobile money operators, banks, card schemes, etc.) We maintain a definitive list of issuers and their codes." cta="Go to issuer reference" href="/concepts/account-issuers">Issuer</Tooltip> of the payment account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment_account_type" type="string">
Type of the payment account.

Available values: `momo`, `card`, `union`
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recipient_account_name" type="string">Name of the recipient account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recipient_account_number" type="string">Number of the recipient account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recipient_account_issuer_name" type="string"><Tooltip headline="Issuers on PeoplesPay" tip="Issuers are the business entities that own payment or recipient accounts (mobile money operators, banks, card schemes, etc.) We maintain a definitive list of issuers and their codes." cta="Go to issuer reference" href="/concepts/account-issuers">Issuer</Tooltip> of the recipient account.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="recipient_account_type" type="string" default="wallet">Type of the recipient account. For collections, this value is `wallet`</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="externalTransactionId" type="string">External transaction identifier, specified by merchant.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="appId" type="string">Identifier of the <Tooltip headline="Apps on PeoplesPay" tip="Apps are API profiles within a merchant account. They have unique IDs and apikeys." cta="Learn more about apps" href="/concepts/merchants"><mark>app</mark></Tooltip> that initiated the transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="callbackUrl" type="string">URL used for transaction callbacks. <Tooltip headline="Webhook events" tip="Webhook events are sent when a collection transaction's status is updated. If you retry the webhook event delivery, it will be sent to value specified in this field." cta="View webhook event reference" href="events/webhook-event-overview">Webhook events</Tooltip> are sent to this location.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="semantic_id" type="string">Semantic identifier for the transaction.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="gtbRef" type="nullable string">
GTB transaction reference.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="payment_account_issuer_name" type="string">Display name of the payment account issuer.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="reason" type="string">Reason accompanying the transaction status.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="debitTimeStamp" type="string">Date and time the payment account was debited, in ISO timestamp format. Determined based on webhook receipt time.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="providerId" type="string">Provider-specific transaction identifier.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="telecelOriginalConversationID" type="string">Original Telecel conversation identifier.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="telecelTransactionID" type="string">Telecel transaction identifier.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="debit_status" type="string">Status of the debit leg of the transaction.</ResponseField>
