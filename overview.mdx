As a payment platform, there are key concepts and entities with the PeoplesPay system that ought to be well-understood.

## Merchants
Merchants refer to businesses that are registered with PeoplesPay and have accounts. Merchants are the only entities that may collect and disburse money.
Merchants have users and apps.

### Users
Merchants can have users under them. These users can log into the merchant portal, and perform any actions as they've been permitted by the main merchant admin.
