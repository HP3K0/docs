The PeoplesPay platform supports bill payments, via a merchant account. The account must be funded to perform these operations. Bill payments operate on a **prepaid** basis.

## Bill categories
Payments can be performed for:
- utilities (water and electric)
- DSTV
- school fee payments
- airtime and data bundles

## Bill payment flow
To complete a bill payment, a number of steps must be followed, with some conditions.

<Tabs>
    <Tab title="General utilities">
        <Info>
            Note that the flow below applies to water utility, TV subscriptions and school fee payments.
        </Info>
        <Steps>
            <Step title="Get services">
                The first step is to query available services. The corresponding `billServiceProviderId`, `billServiceProductId` must be included in the subsequent request.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Lookup account">
                The account that is to be credited must be looked up. The returned `sessionId` is required for the commit step.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Commit">
                The payment is initiated.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Webhook call">
                The requester receives an HTTP notification at the specified webhook URL.
            </Step>
        </Steps> 
    </Tab>
    <Tab title="ECG">
        <Steps>
            <Step title="Get services">
                The first step is to query available services. The corresponding `billServiceProviderId`, `billServiceProductId` must be included in the subsequent request.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Lookup meter">
                The meter that is to be credited must be looked up. The returned `sessionId` is required for the commit step.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Check meter status">
                The meter status must be checked, to ensure its current availability.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Commit">
                The payment is initiated.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Webhook call">
                The requester receives an HTTP notification at the specified webhook URL.
            </Step>
        </Steps>        
    </Tab>
    <Tab title="Airtime">
        <Steps>
            <Step title="Initiate purchase">
                Specify the network, the phone number and amount, then fire the API call.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Webhook call">
                The requester receives an HTTP notification at the specified webhook URL.
            </Step>
        </Steps>               
    </Tab>
    <Tab title="Data bundles">
        <Steps>
            <Step title="Query available data bundles">
                Check for available data bundles.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Initiate purchase">
                Specify the network, the phone number and amount, then fire the API call.
            </Step>
            <Step title="Webhook call">
                The requester receives an HTTP notification at the specified webhook URL.
            </Step>
        </Steps>
    </Tab>
</Tabs>



## Management
Bill payment transactions show up with a `transactionType` of `PB` in tranasaction history. Airtime topups have a `transactionType` of `AT` As such, look out for those entries when [retrieving transaction history](/api-reference/endpoint/get-transactions).
