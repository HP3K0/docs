Bill payments in PeoplesPay are made possible through an aggregator, ITC. To perform a bill payment, you'll need to specify the billers corresponding information, along with that of the payee.

## Bill categories
Payments can be performed for:
- utilities
- TV
- school fees

## Management
Bill payment transactions show up with a `transactionType` of `PB` in tranasaction history.
