Welcome to PeoplesPay! This guide will take you through the basics of collecting money (into your PeoplesPay account) as well as disbursing money (sending money to a customer from your PeoplesPay account).
This quickstart outlines these operations via the PeoplesPay API, so you will need to have access to that first.

## Prerequisites
<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Approved merchant account">
    The final step of the PeoplesPay onboarding process involves approval. If you've submitted all requested documents, that final step may not have been completed, especially when review takes longer than anticipated. Ensure you've been approved before going ahead to perform the operations in this guide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="API access">
    API access requires the use of an API key. You will find your API key by accessing the [merchant portal](https://merchants.peoplespay.com.gh)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Collecting money
Collections are the primary method to credit a PeoplesPay account (used interchangeably with *wallet*). Collections involve the transfer of money from an external account, either a mobile money wallet or a bank card, to a specified PeoplesPay account.
Virtually all PeoplesPay accounts can perform collections, which isn't the case for disbursements. A collection is a type of *transaction*; on the merchant portal, collections have a `transactionType` of `RM`. 

<Note>
  If you cannot perfom a collection, and your account has not been disabled, try contacting [support](mailto:info@peoplespay.com.gh). 
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a collections access token">
    Send a POST request to generate an access token to perform a collection. Specify the `merchantId`, `apikey` and `operation` in the request body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perform a collections request">
    Send a POST request containing all required information for the collections transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize payment (non-card)">
    The customer authorizes payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Check your PeoplesPay account balance and transaction history for the relevant collections transaction. The initial status of the transaction will be **pending**, which is eventually resolved to **paid** or **failed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Disbursing money
Disbursements are very similar to collections. The main steps are the same, and the outcome is the same. The following points are of note, however:
- **failed** disbursements will result in a balance deduction by the corresponding transaction amount.
- access to disbursement is not available to all PeoplesPay accounts. If this is the case for you, reach out to support.
- disbursements can be performed into mobile money wallets and/or bank accounts.
