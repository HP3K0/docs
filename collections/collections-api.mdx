Collections are the primary method to credit a PeoplesPay account. Money may be collected via mobile money or bank card.

## Authentication
The first step in a PeoplesPay collection is token generation. By calling the [/hub/token/get](/api-reference/endpoint/get-token) endpoint
with the correct payload, the platform issues a time-limited access token that authorizes subsequent collections requests. This JWT access token
encodes the merchant's `appId` and the type of operation.

## Payment methods
<Info>
The request payload varies based on the collection's method of payment.
    
</Info>
<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion
title="Mobile money"
>
 Mobile money collections have peculiar considerations. The exact flow looks something like this:
 <Steps>
    <Step title="Initialisation">
        The collection is initialised as the POST request is sent to the momo collections [endpoint](/api-reference/endpoint/collect-momo).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Capture">
        The PeoplesPay platform communicates with the corresponding network provider's servers to trigger an payment prompt on the payer's phone
    </Step>
    <Step title="Approval">
        The payer approves (or declines) the payment
    </Step>
    <Step title="Notification">
        The network provider's systems notify the PeoplesPay platform of the collection outcome
    </Step>
 </Steps>
</Accordion>
<Accordion
title="Card"
>
 Collections performed by bank card are quite different to mobile money collections. The flow is as follows:
 <Steps>
    <Step title="Initialisation">
        The collection is initialised as the POST request is sent to the card collections [endpoint](/api-reference/endpoint/collect-card).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Card scheme gateway">
        The PeoplesPay platform communicates with a card scheme gateway to proceed
    </Step>
    <Step title="Approval">
        The payer approves (or declines) the payment
    </Step>
    <Step title="Notification">
        The network provider's systems notify the PeoplesPay platform of the collection outcome
    </Step>
 </Steps>
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
