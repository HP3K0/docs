<Badge color="red">Synchronous flow</Badge>

Using the merchant portal, you can perform bulk disbursements.

## Access the merchant portal
Go to the merchant portal, log in and head to the **Bulk Transfer** page, under **Bulk Disburse**.
![image](/images/merchant-disburse.png)


## Performing the disbursement
 The disbursement flow is as follows:
 <Steps>
    <Step title="Define recipients">
        Follow the instructions on the page to add your list of recipients, for your bulk disbursement. You'll have to download a CSV template from the page and enter the recipient details.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Upload and verify">
        Upload the filled-in template, and verify recipients.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Initiate">
        After reviewing all the transaction details, go ahead to initiate the disbursement.
    </Step>
 </Steps>
