## Prerequisites
<Accordion title="Complete onboarding">
You need to have completed onboarding, with a fully active merchant instance on PeoplesPay and a signed agreement to proceed with integration.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Merchant portal access">

## Technical considerations
Once you have a created app and an API key, you may begin integration. Use the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction) and read up on how the platform works to integrate properly and to avoid potential pitfalls.
<Info>
If at any time you require assistance, speak to your onboarding manager, or [contact support](mailto:ppsupport@bsystemslimited.com).

## Live transaction testing
Live transaction testing is a crucial aspect of integration. It ensures production-environment alignment through executing small-value transactions. Here's what to look out for:

- account balance before and after completing transactions
- correctly receiving and processing webhook notifications (e.g. to update transaction statuses)
- passing merchant-generated transaction IDs under `externalTransactionId`
- executing transactions in different modes (e.g. simultaneous transactions, out-of-bounds values, exceeding balances, etc.)
- receiving USSD prompts for mobile money collections
- verifying recipient names before performing disbursements


<Note>
If you need payment or receipient account details to run tests, speak to your onboarding manager.
</Note>
